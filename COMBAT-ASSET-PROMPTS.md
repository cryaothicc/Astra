# Combat presentation revision

All images generated with the built-in image generation tool. Final files are in `public/assets/`: `operators-attack.png` (5×3), `boss-warden.png`, `boss-frost.png`, and `boss-cinder.png` (4×3 each). Boss rows: walking, attack, defeat. The Warden and Cinder sheets received a second background-extraction pass to make the gutters fully transparent, preserving all frames. The renderer registers transparent gutters and feet at load time rather than cutting weapons at assumed column boundaries. Biome enemy and shrine prompts are in MAP-PROMPTS.md.

## operatorsCleanPrompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Production game sprite atlas, genuinely transparent background. Use reference only for the five character identities and smooth painted anime chibi style. EXACTLY FIVE COLUMNS and THREE ROWS, 15 separated full-body sprites in equal cells. All characters and all weapons face RIGHT consistently in every single cell. Column1 white-haired cat-eared girl Ash with orange scarf and short spear; column2 brown-haired defender man Bastion with shield; column3 red-haired ponytail sniper girl Kestrel with crossbow; column4 violet-haired glasses female caster Lumen with staff; column5 mint-haired tan-skinned medic Suture with healing gun. Row1 anticipation, row2 powerful physical attack pose, row3 follow-through recovery. Animate the arms, legs, spine, hair and clothing; dramatic but planted boots. Same scale across each character's three frames. Crucial: NO magic, NO projectiles, NO glow, NO muzzle flashes, NO energy streaks, NO shadows, NO lettering. Effects will be rendered separately by game engine. Reserve at least 18% empty padding on all sides of EACH CELL; no weapon, hair, cape, scarf or foot touching cell boundaries. Every sprite completely isolated with generous transparent space, never overlapping neighboring cells. Sprite foot baseline at 86% cell height. Keep all characters proportionally small enough that the spear and staff fit safely inside the cell. Landscape 5:3 atlas.
