# Asset generation

All three project assets were made with the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool, using the user-supplied screenshot as the reference. No CLI/API-key fallback was used. Generated alpha was preserved. Atlas frames are sampled at runtime rather than destructively edited. The supplied screenshot remains unmodified.

## Battlefield prompt

Keep the exact composition, camera perspective, grey industrial concrete tiled battlefield, railings, walls, crates, raised square platforms, markings, road path, red entrance left and cyan blue exit right, lighting and richly detailed anime game painting style unchanged. REMOVE all 6 chibi characters on the battlefield, all health bars, all cyan highlighted floor tiles, cyan square selection borders, gunshot tracer lines, attack sparks. Fill their former locations seamlessly with the concrete floor or the road or the platform surface underneath. Also remove the complete TOP HUD and BOTTOM operator card UI by replacing the top 60px and bottom 225px of the original 1672x941 frame with plain almost-black #1d2023 panels. Preserve the entire battlefield framing (it occupies y60..710 of the reference) and maintain its relative position in the full 16:9 image. This should look exactly like the empty version of the reference map, no extra design changes. No characters, text, UI icons, or health bars. Output a wide 16:9 image.
